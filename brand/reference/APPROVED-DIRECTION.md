# Approved Cognita Direction

Status: Approved current brand direction

## Core identity

Cognita is positioned as a premium Filipino AI learning institution with a serious, credible, future-facing visual system.

Primary message:

> Human potential amplified by AI.

Supporting line:

> Real skills. Real Guidance. A more capable Philippines.

Learning framework:

> THINK. APPLY. TRANSFORM.

## Art direction name

**Matte Holographic Institutional System**

Alternative descriptive label: **Cinematic Institutional Futurism**.

The objective is not to look like a generic AI startup. Cognita should feel like a high-trust technology institute or innovation center: academically grounded, commercially polished, Filipino in purpose, and technologically advanced.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Institutional authority
2. Human capability
3. Intelligent technology
4. Practical learning
5. Filipino progress

Technology is the visual language, not the subject of empty decoration.

## Holographic system

Use overlapping translucent planes as a recurring Cognita motif. They may represent readiness, CEE, AI-00, programs, evidence, verification, pathways, or human review.

Panels should:

- use frosted/translucent matte glass
- overlap at different depths
- use restrained blue illumination
- occasionally use a warm-gold edge or line
- cross section boundaries in high-impact compositions when appropriate
- retain readable hierarchy and accessibility
- become quieter in long-form institutional sections

Avoid glossy plastic, chrome, aggressive bloom, gaming HUD language, excessive particles, spinning objects, or random sci-fi symbols.

## Surface and finish

The approved finish is matte.

- low sheen
- diffused reflections
- restrained light bloom
- deep navy architectural surfaces
- cool, slightly desaturated blue illumination
- brushed/champagne warm-gold detail
- subtle texture or grain where useful
- soft physical-looking shadows

The aim is a photographed premium installation, not a synthetic render.

## Light and dark balance

The system is not an all-dark theme and not an all-white theme.

Use dark/navy environments for the hero and major high-impact moments. Use Ivory/light sections for programs, admissions, policy, long-form reading, and other content where clarity should dominate. Maintain enough contrast between sections to create rhythm.

## Imagery

For the primary brand and homepage hero:

- no people required
- no generic stock portraits
- no AI-generated faces
- no robots or humanoid AI imagery
- no mountain/travel imagery used as the core concept

The Philippines may be visualized through a refined map, network, geographic abstraction, or other meaningful national reference. Any map must be geographically responsible and should not be treated as decorative tourism imagery.

## Logo

The approved logo is the COGNITA wordmark. The current system has no separate icon.

Do not revive the superseded multicolor icon or recreate alternative marks without approval.

The formal descriptor `INSTITUTE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE` may appear below the wordmark for institutional applications.

## Color discipline

- Deep Navy `#0B1F3B`: trust and institutional authority
- Midnight Blue `#102A56`: depth and stability
- Luminous Blue `#3B82F6`: intelligence and innovation
- Ivory `#F8FAFC`: clarity and simplicity
- Cool Gray `#94A3B8`: balance and secondary information
- Warm Gold `#D4AF7C`: opportunity and human potential

Gold should remain approximately 5–10% of a composition and should normally appear as rules, edges, buttons, highlights, or small typographic accents.

## Typography

- Tiempos Headline for editorial/display typography where licensing allows
- Inter for UI, body, data, and navigation

Do not add proprietary font files to the repository.

## Content guardrails

Do not publish invented or unverified metrics such as learner counts, job outcomes, application rates, mentor totals, placements, partner counts, or geographic reach.

Do not use decorative data visualizations that can be mistaken for real institutional performance data.

Any institutional claim must match Cognita's actual legal, academic, operational, and regulatory status.

## Brand attributes

Intelligent. Human-centered. Premium. Practical. Credible. Progressive.

These attributes should be visible in the work rather than repeatedly written as marketing copy.
