## Canonical rule

Cognita is a commercial private non-degree professional training and competency-development business.

Number-one objective: **sustainable revenue and profit.**

Canonical product flow:
**Create Account → CEE → Learner Profile → Gap Analysis → Personalized Pathway → Learning Mode Recommendation → Paid Offer → Training → Mastery → Evidence → Verified Competency → Next Paid Path**

Do not restore older application-first, invitation-only, pass/fail-first, or catalog-first logic.

## CEE

CEE = **Cognita Entry & Competency Evaluation**.

It determines competency, technical readiness, educational support needs, goals/constraints, pathway, specialization, learning mode, support intensity, and paid-offer recommendation.

Learner Readiness & Support Profile is non-clinical.

Do not call it a psychological examination or diagnose psychological/mental-health conditions.

## Commercial copy

Primary hook:
**Stop taking AI courses you may not need.**

Support:
**Take the CEE. Discover your AI level. Get the learning path built for you.**

Long-term:
**Don't just learn AI. Prove you can use it.**

Use persuasive but truthful conversion design. No fake scarcity, false guarantees, hidden charges, or vulnerability-based pricing.

## Compliance

Cognita is a private, non-degree provider.

Do not claim CHED/DepEd/TESDA/PRC/government recognition or credentials unless actually obtained and applicable.

CEE profiling requires privacy-by-design, clear notice/consent where required, appropriate registrations, security, retention controls, and a review route.

Learner work remains learner-owned unless a separate written commercialization agreement says otherwise.

## Frontend-only

The repository remains frontend-only until secure production identity, assessment, payment, records, and privacy infrastructure are approved.

Do not represent local simulations as live backend services.

Use the committed Cognita brand system for all visual changes.
