# PART I — Institutional and Regulatory Positioning

## 1. Nature of Cognita

Cognita is intended to operate as a **private, non-degree professional training and competency-development provider**.

Cognita provides structured training, assessment, personalized learning pathways, professional development, AI-assisted learning services, projects, feedback, competency verification, and related products for individuals and organizations.

Unless expressly stated for a specific authorized program, Cognita does not represent that it is:
- a university or college
- a formal basic-education school
- a government certification authority
- a professional licensing authority
- a CHED degree or academic-credit provider
- a DepEd-recognized school
- a TESDA qualification/National Certificate/Certificate of Competency provider
- a PRC licensing provider

Any future accreditation, registration, recognition, or authorization must be identified specifically and must not be generalized to unrelated programs.

## 2. TVET/TESDA review

Before launching any offering that may fall within a regulated TVET category, obtain a documented legal/compliance determination and complete any required program registration.

Calling a program "private," "non-degree," or "certificate" is not by itself a regulatory exemption.

## 3. CEE psychological-services boundary

The CEE Learner Readiness & Support Profile is educational and non-clinical.

Do not market it as:
- psychological examination
- clinical psychological assessment
- mental-health diagnosis
- regulated psychometric service

unless appropriately licensed professionals, compliant instruments, and required procedures are actually used.

Payment may be processed by Cognita or authorized payment providers.

Recurring billing requires a separate explicit authorization stating the amount and cadence.

Example:
**I authorize recurring charges of ₱[AMOUNT] every [PERIOD] until I cancel.**

## 15. Receipts and records

Provide required transaction confirmation, invoice, receipt, or equivalent record.

## 16. Program access

Access may be fixed-term, cohort-based, subscription-based, or otherwise expressly stated.

"Lifetime" must never be used more broadly than the actual offer.

## 17. Personalized pathways

Pathways may change when new evidence appears, prerequisites are missing, mastery checks fail, competence is demonstrated early, technology changes, curriculum is updated, or safety/compliance reasons require revision.

## 18. Self-Paced

Self-Paced means schedule flexibility, not reduced competency standards.

Prerequisite, mastery, project, remediation, and assessment requirements may still apply.

## 19. Guided

Guided may include structured milestones, mentor/facilitator feedback, project review, accountability, synchronous sessions, cohort/community support, office hours, and capstone defense as described in the purchased offer.

## 20. Scheduling

Cognita may reasonably reschedule guided sessions because of emergencies, severe weather, instructor availability, technical failures, power interruption, or comparable operational issues, with reasonable alternative arrangements where practicable.

## 21. Connectivity/equipment

Unless expressly provided, learners are responsible for compatible devices, connectivity, and required software.

Material technical requirements should be disclosed before purchase.

## 22. Third-party tools

Training may use independent AI, productivity, cloud, video, or development services subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Cognita may substitute tools when availability changes while preserving the relevant learning objective where practicable.

## 23. AI-assisted services

Cognita may use AI to support assessment, tutoring, feedback, practice generation, pathway recommendation, learner support, analytics, and operations.

AI outputs can be inaccurate. High-stakes decisions should use appropriate human review.

## 24. Assessment integrity

Cognita may invalidate work or credentials for substantiated impersonation, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, prohibited outsourcing, assessment manipulation, hacking, or material academic dishonesty.

Where practicable, provide an internal review route before permanent credential revocation.

## 25. Learner AI use

AI use is not automatically prohibited.

Each assessment defines permitted and prohibited assistance.

Learners may be required to explain, defend, or reproduce submitted work.

## 26. Mastery

Completion of content or attendance does not prove competence.

Cognita may require quizzes, scenarios, practical tasks, projects, oral explanation, demonstrations, capstones, revisions, remediation, and retesting.

## 27. Credentials

Cognita may issue proprietary private credentials such as:
- Certificate of Completion
- Cognita Competency Record
- Cognita Verified Competency Record
- pathway credentials
- digital badges

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, these are private Cognita credentials, not degrees, professional licenses, or government-issued qualifications.

No third party is required to recognize a Cognita credential.

## 28. Credential verification/revocation

Cognita may retain minimal verification data such as name, credential ID, issue date, title, competency category, status, and revocation status.

Credentials may be corrected or revoked for proven fraud, identity substitution, assessment manipulation, or material administrative error, subject to an appropriate review process.

# PART III — Intellectual Property and Learner Work

## 29. Learner-created work

Learners retain ownership of original work unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Submission grants Cognita a limited non-exclusive license to store, reproduce internally, evaluate, annotate, process for assessment, perform integrity checks, maintain academic records, and verify credentials.

Ordinary enrollment does not authorize Cognita to commercially sell learner-owned work.

## 30. Commercialization agreement

Commercial sale, licensing, publication, incubation, marketplace use, employer placement, or other commercial exploitation of learner-owned work requires a separate written agreement addressing:
- ownership
- license scope
- exclusivity
- revenue share/payment
- attribution
- derivative works
- confidentiality
- warranties
- taxes
- termination

Participation should not automatically be required to earn an ordinary credential.

## 31. Portfolio/testimonial permission

Public display of private learner work, testimonials, photographs, videos, or success stories should use a separate lawful permission where required.

Declining optional promotional permission should not invalidate an otherwise earned credential.

## 32. Cognita IP

Cognita retains rights in assessments, question banks, scoring systems, routing logic, competency frameworks, instructional materials, videos, graphics, templates, rubrics, databases, software, platform designs, trademarks, branding, proprietary prompts/workflows, and credential formats.

Purchase grants only the limited use rights stated for the training.

## 33. Prohibited reproduction

Users may not resell paid materials, publish protected assessments/answer keys, sell accounts, scrape protected content, systematically copy paid courses, or use Cognita IP to create a substantially copied competing training product without authorization.

- support/complaint records: generally up to 3 years after closure
- accounting/payment records: required legal period
- minimal credential verification: while verification remains necessary
- de-identified analytics: longer where re-identification is not reasonably possible
