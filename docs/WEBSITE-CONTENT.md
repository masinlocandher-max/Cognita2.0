## About Cognita Institute

Cognita Institute is a private training and learning institution designed for Filipino learners.

We believe quality education should be structured, practical, accessible, and connected to the realities of the people it serves. Cognita develops learning experiences that help students strengthen foundational knowledge, build practical skills, and progress with greater confidence toward academic, professional, and personal goals.

Our approach combines self-paced learning with structured assessments, clear progression standards, and appropriate human guidance. Students are expected to demonstrate understanding and competency, not simply complete lessons.

Cognita is built around the needs of Filipino learners. This means considering different educational backgrounds, levels of English proficiency, access to technology, learning pace, and the practical demands students face outside the classroom.

We aim to create an environment where learning remains flexible without losing rigor, and where progress is measured through genuine understanding and demonstrated capability.

At Cognita, admission is intentional, learning is structured, and progress is earned.

## Mission

Cognita Institute exists to provide Filipino learners with structured, accessible, and competency-based training that develops practical knowledge, strengthens foundational skills, and prepares them for meaningful academic, professional, and personal growth.

# View the Admission Process

## Your journey at Cognita begins with admission.

Cognita Institute follows a structured admission process designed to help ensure that applicants are ready for the learning experience and are placed appropriately before enrollment.

### Step 1 — Submit Your Application

Complete the Cognita application form and provide the required applicant information.

Your application allows Cognita to review your basic eligibility and determine whether you may proceed to the next stage of admission.

CTA: **Begin Your Application**

### Step 2 — Application Review

After submission, your application will be reviewed.

Applicants who meet the initial admission requirements will receive instructions through their registered email address.

Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and that you regularly check your email for updates regarding your application.

### Step 3 — Receive Your CEE Access

Approved applicants will receive access to the Cognita Entrance Examination, or CEE, through email.

Your examination access is intended only for you and must not be shared with another person.

The CEE is timed and includes integrity safeguards intended to help maintain a fair and credible admission process.

### Step 4 — Take the Cognita Entrance Examination

Complete the CEE within the instructions and conditions provided.

The examination helps Cognita assess your current readiness and foundational competencies.

Applicants are expected to complete the examination independently and honestly.

### Step 5 — Receive Your Admission and Pathway Result

After your examination has been evaluated, Cognita will send your admission result to your registered email address.

Passing applicants may also receive an academic pathway recommendation based on readiness evidence:

- Foundation Required
- Foundation Accelerated
- Direct Track Entry

The recommendation helps determine whether additional foundational support through AI-00 is appropriate before or alongside the selected learning route.

### Step 6 — Choose Your Program

Successful applicants may explore the programs currently available for enrollment.

Cognita may recommend a guided or self-paced pathway based on the learner's readiness, goals, and need for structure.

CTA: **Explore Our Programs**

### Step 7 — Complete Payment and Enrollment

Once you have selected your program, proceed with the required enrollment and payment process.

Your enrollment is considered complete only after the required steps have been successfully confirmed.

### Step 8 — Create Your Student Account

After enrollment confirmation, you will be instructed to create your Cognita student account.

This account provides access to your official learning environment.

### Step 9 — Enter the Cognita Learning App

Once your account is activated, you may access the Cognita Learning App.

This is where you will find your lessons, activities, assessments, learning progress, program requirements, and other student resources.

Your learning journey officially begins here.

## Before You Apply

Please make sure that:

- you provide complete and accurate information;
- you have access to a working email address;
- you have a suitable device and internet connection for the entrance examination and online learning;
- you are prepared to follow Cognita's academic-integrity and student policies;
- you understand that admission does not automatically guarantee completion or certification.

Cognita evaluates learning through participation where applicable, assessment, demonstrated understanding, required outputs, and fulfillment of program requirements.

Take the first step toward becoming a Cognita learner.

# Explore Our Programs

## Learning designed for Filipino learners.

Cognita Institute develops structured training programs that help learners build practical knowledge, strengthen foundational skills, and develop capabilities they can apply beyond the classroom.

Our programs combine flexible learning with clear standards, guided progression, assessments, and measurable learning outcomes.

## Two primary ways to learn

### Cognita Professional AI Program

**10 Weeks · Guided · Cohort-Based · Mentor-Supported**

The Cognita Professional AI Program is the Institute's flagship guided learning experience.

It is designed for learners who benefit from structure, deadlines, live guidance, human feedback, cohort accountability, and an intensive applied-learning environment.

The academic model combines a foundation layer with a six-week specialization. CEE readiness evidence can be used to determine whether a learner needs the full foundation, an accelerated foundation, or is ready for direct track entry.

Initial specialization tracks:

- AI for Students
- AI for Creatives
- AI for Entrepreneurs
- AI for Professionals & Virtual Assistants

The program emphasizes real outputs, mentor review, revision, portfolio evidence, and a capstone. Completion is based on demonstrated competency rather than attendance alone.

Suggested CTA: **View the 10-Week Program**

### Cognita Skills Lab

**Self-Paced · Project-Based · Assessment-Driven**

**Applied AI Foundations and Professional Practice**

Core promise: **Learn it. Build it. Prove it.**

Cognita Skills Lab is designed for independent learners who need greater control over when and how they study without lowering the academic standard required to complete the program.

Recommended duration: **28 days**

Estimated learning time: **32–40 hours**

Learners may move faster or slower than the recommended schedule.

The eight-module core covers:

1. AI Foundations and Reality Check
2. Problem Framing and Strategic Thinking
3. Prompt Design and Instruction Quality
4. Research, Verification, and Evidence
5. AI-Assisted Professional Workflows
6. Communication, Creativity, and Quality Control
7. Ethics, Privacy, Bias, and Intellectual Property
8. Capstone Development and Professional Defense

Self-paced learners can pause, resume, repeat lessons, and resubmit selected exercises. Required outputs and assessment standards must still be satisfied before a credential is earned.

Suggested CTA: **View the Self-Paced Program**

## How Learning Works

### Self-Paced, With Structure

Students in self-paced learning can progress through materials according to their own schedule while still meeting required lessons, activities, assessments, outputs, and competency checks.

### Guided, With Accountability

Students in the 10-week Professional AI Program follow a structured cohort schedule with mentor/facilitator support, deadlines, applied work, and feedback.

### Assessment-Based Progression

Progress is based on demonstrated learning, not simply time spent inside the platform.

Assessments may include quizzes, written activities, practical exercises, projects, examinations, professional outputs, capstones, or other forms of competency evaluation depending on the program.

### Human Guidance When It Matters

Technology supports the Cognita learning experience, but it does not replace appropriate human oversight.

Certain activities, assessments, learner concerns, integrity issues, or academic decisions may require human review or intervention.

### Built for Filipino Learners

Cognita programs are developed with consideration for the realities of Filipino learners, including differences in educational background, English proficiency, access to technology, learning pace, and responsibilities outside formal study.

Where appropriate, Cognita may provide foundational support before a learner progresses to more advanced material.
