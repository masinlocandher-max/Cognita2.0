## CEE page

### CEE: Cognita Entry & Competency Evaluation

The first step is not choosing a course.

It is understanding what you actually need.

The CEE evaluates current competency, technical readiness, learning-support needs, goals, and practical constraints.

It can influence:
- starting level
- competencies to study
- competencies you may skip
- remediation
- specialization
- Self-Paced vs Guided recommendation
- support intensity
- recommended paid Cognita offering

### Learner Readiness & Support Profile

The CEE may ask about study consistency, accountability preferences, confidence, response to difficult tasks, time management, and preferred participation style.

This is an educational readiness profile.

It is **not a clinical psychological or mental-health diagnosis.**

## Learning Modes

### Self-Paced
For independent learners who need flexibility.

You control the schedule while still meeting Cognita's competency, assessment, project, and mastery requirements.

### Guided
For learners who are likely to perform better with structure, checkpoints, accountability, human feedback, and support.

Guided is a premium option, but it should be recommended because the learner profile supports it, not simply because it costs more.

### Organizations
**Don't train everyone the same.**

Cognita can assess participant capability, identify shared and individual gaps, design a cohort pathway, train what matters, and verify the resulting capability.

## Mastery

At Cognita, completing lessons is not the same as developing competence.

Typical loop:
**Learn → Do → Feedback → Improve → Retry → Master**

Projects and assessments create evidence of what the learner can actually do.

## Credential

A Cognita credential represents demonstrated evidence within Cognita's private professional training system.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, it is not a degree, professional license, or government-issued qualification.

## Trust

Cognita does not promise guaranteed jobs, guaranteed income, instant mastery, or fake outcomes.

The promise is more defensible:
**We assess where you are, train what you need, and verify what you can demonstrate.**

## Private training disclosure

Cognita is a private, non-degree professional training and competency-development provider.

Government recognition, accreditation, academic credit, or professional licensing status will be claimed only when actually obtained and specifically applicable.
