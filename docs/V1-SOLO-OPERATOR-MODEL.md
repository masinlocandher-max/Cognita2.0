# Cognita V1 — Solo-Operator Commercial Model

Status: Canonical operating model for a small pilot
Updated: 2026-09-28

## Purpose

Cognita V1 should be operable by one founder/operator for a deliberately small pilot without inventing staff.

Core principle:
**One operator. Separate functions. Recorded decisions. No invented staff.**

## Number-one objective

Operate the smallest credible version that can convert paying learners, deliver strong outcomes, gather evidence, and validate the economics.

Avoid building administrative complexity that does not improve revenue, compliance, learner outcomes, or operating control.

## Canonical customer flow

**Acquire → Create Account → CEE → Profile → Personalized Path → Recommended Offer → Payment → Training → Mastery → Credential → Next Offer / Renewal**

Do not restore application-first admissions review as the universal commercial front door.

## Founder/operator functions

One person may perform:
- Founder / Institutional Lead
- CEE/Pathway Reviewer
- Trainer / Facilitator
- Student Support
- Records / Enrollment Administration
- Commercial / Institutional Sales

These are functions, not claims of separate employees.

## Human-review priorities

Human time should be concentrated on:
- CEE recommendation challenges or ambiguous cases
- high-value Guided feedback
- capstone/evidence review
- learner support that cannot be automated safely
- credential quality control
- institutional sales and renewal
- significant complaints, integrity issues, or disputes

## Automation priority

Automate or systematize where reliable:
- account onboarding
- CEE scoring components
- pathway assembly
- routine recommendations
- reminders
- progress tracking
- standard feedback patterns
- payment/enrollment status once production infrastructure exists
- agreement/version logging
- credential verification

Automation must not bypass required legal/privacy or human-review safeguards.

## Commercial metrics for pilot

Track:
- account-to-CEE completion
- CEE-to-paid conversion
- Self-Paced vs Guided mix
- revenue per learner
- delivery hours per learner
- gross margin
- completion/mastery
- support load
- refund rate
- upsell/next-path rate
- referrals
- institutional leads and contract value

## Scale boundary

Do not scale learner volume faster than:
- feedback capacity
- support capacity
- privacy/security controls
- payment/records reliability
- credential quality
- instructor/facilitator capacity

Before material scale, add independent review for serious disputes, appeals, credential revocation, and conflicts of interest.

## Frontend-only limitation

Current operations screens are local simulations only.

They are not production staff portals, authentication, payment, audit, or learner-record systems.
