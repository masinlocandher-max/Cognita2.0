## Product objective

Cognita is a commercial private non-degree professional training and competency-development business.

Number-one objective:
**sustainable revenue and profit.**

The frontend must make the differentiated product model obvious.

## Canonical journey

**Create Account → CEE → Competency Profile → Gap Analysis → Personalized Path → Recommended Learning Mode → Paid Offer → Training → Mastery → Evidence → Verified Competency → Next Paid Path**

Do not restore application-first admissions, invitation-only CEE, pass/fail-first routing, or account-after-payment.

## Public hook

Primary:
**Stop taking AI courses you may not need.**

Support:
**Take the CEE. Discover your AI level. Get the learning path built for you.**

Long-term:
**Don't just learn AI. Prove you can use it.**

## CEE

CEE = Cognita Entry & Competency Evaluation.

The result should feel valuable and personalized, not like a simple pass/fail score.

It should explain:
- current capability
- strongest areas
- gaps
- what can be skipped
- recommended depth
- specialization
- Self-Paced vs Guided
- recommended paid offer
- why

Learner Readiness & Support Profile is educational and non-clinical.

Do not label or diagnose users psychologically.

## Learning modes

Self-Paced: flexible, lower-touch, same competency standard.

Guided: premium, more structure, accountability, feedback/support, and checkpoints.

Organizations: assessment-led cohort training.

## Conversion

Use truthful personalization, curiosity, uncertainty reduction, progress framing, and clear recommendations.

Do not use fake scarcity, fake countdowns, hidden charges, false job/income guarantees, or vulnerability-based pricing.

## Legal/public trust

Cognita is private and non-degree.

Do not claim CHED, DepEd, TESDA, PRC, government accreditation/certification, academic credit, or licensing unless actually obtained and specifically applicable.

Material privacy, profiling, billing, refund, and recurring-payment information must be clear.

## Frontend-only boundary

The repository does not yet provide secure production identity, CEE, payment, records, or credential infrastructure.

Do not make a fake account form or fake checkout look production-live.

Design the final production UX, but clearly separate simulation from live capability.

## Visual direction

Use the committed Cognita brand system.

The public experience should be premium, institutional, modern, human, and credible.

Avoid generic SaaS/dashboard aesthetics, fake metrics, excessive glassmorphism, and decorative AI clichés.
