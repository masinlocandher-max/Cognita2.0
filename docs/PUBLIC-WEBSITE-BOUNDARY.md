# Cognita 2.0 — Public Website Boundary

Status: LOCKED frontend direction for the current build phase
Updated: 2026-09-28

## Purpose

The public website explains Cognita's private professional training model, CEE, personalized pathways, learning modes, institutional training, legal positioning, policies, founder, and contact information.

The final product architecture is account-first:
**Create Account → CEE → Profile → Personalized Path → Recommended Offer → Payment → Learning**

The current repository remains frontend-only. Therefore the site must not falsely represent account creation, secure CEE submission, payment, authentication, cloud records, or credential issuance as production-live before the required backend and compliance systems exist.

Program/catalog browsing may exist as supporting information, but the primary commercial route should be CEE-led rather than catalog-led.

## Future production transactional routes

When secure production infrastructure is approved:
- /account/create
- /cee/start
- /cee/result
- /path
- /checkout
- /app

These routes must not be production-enabled until secure server-side identity, authorization, assessment protection, payment, records, and privacy controls are implemented.

## Development-only routes

Local simulations may exist for QA, including CEE, payment, learner, or operations screens.

They are not production access-control systems.

Hiding a link is not security.

## Primary public CTA

Where production account creation is not yet live:
**How the CEE Works** or a truthful waitlist/contact action.

When production account creation is live:
**Create My Account**

Do not use a fake account form that implies secure registration when no backend exists.

## Website positioning

Primary hook:
**Stop taking AI courses you may not need.**

Support:
**Take the CEE. Discover your AI level. Get the learning path built for you.**

Long-term:
**Don't just learn AI. Prove you can use it.**

## Public CEE explanation

CEE = **Cognita Entry & Competency Evaluation**.

Explain that CEE is used to:
- assess current competency
- identify gaps
- determine what can be skipped
- recommend depth and specialization
- recommend Self-Paced or Guided
- determine support intensity
- recommend an appropriate Cognita paid offering

The Learner Readiness & Support Profile is educational and non-clinical.

## Policies

The public site should provide readable versions of:
- Terms of Service / Enrollment Agreement
- Privacy Notice
- CEE profiling and AI disclosure
- refund/cancellation policy
- academic integrity / learner AI-use rules
- credential disclosure
- institutional/regulatory status
- complaints/redress
- accessibility/support information

Material terms must be clear and not intentionally buried.

## Checkout

Before payment show:
- product
- learning mode
- price
- billing cadence
- access period
- material limitations
- short refund summary

Recurring billing requires separate explicit authorization.

## Legal positioning

Cognita is a **private, non-degree professional training and competency-development provider**.

Do not imply CHED, DepEd, TESDA, PRC, government academic credit, certification, accreditation, or licensing status unless actually obtained and specifically applicable.

## Contact

Final production ecommerce/contact disclosures must also include the actual legal entity, business address, and other required contact information once finalized.

## Visual credibility

Maintain the existing Cognita institutional brand system.

The website may be commercially strong without looking like a generic SaaS dashboard.

Use calm authority, clear hierarchy, evidence, strong calls to action, and direct explanations.

Avoid fake metrics, fake scarcity, fake countdowns, exaggerated earnings claims, or manipulative learner labels.
