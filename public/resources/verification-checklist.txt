COGNITA VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
Public practice resource. Not a formal assessment.
1. List consequential claims: names, dates, prices, promises, quantities.
2. Identify the original authorized source for each claim.
3. Check that the source supports the exact claim.
4. Recalculate numerical results independently.
5. Separate revenue from profit and estimates from observed results.
6. Record missing facts and unresolved uncertainty.
7. Remove unsupported claims or ask for clarification.
8. Identify who approves the output before use.
