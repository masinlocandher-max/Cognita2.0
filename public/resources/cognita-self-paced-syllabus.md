# Cognita Self-Paced Syllabus

Applied AI for Everyday Work (SP-AI-01) | v1.0.0

Status: curriculum prepared for pilot review. Enrollment is not yet open in this build.

Audience: Adults developing AI literacy and entry-level applied AI skills. No coding prerequisite.

## Learning outcomes
- Separate useful assistance from decisions that need human judgment.
- Write a usable brief while excluding unnecessary personal information.
- Develop and test instructions against explicit criteria.
- Trace important claims to evidence and recompute calculations.
- Create a repeatable process with failure handling and approval.
- Edit a draft for audience, evidence, and a useful next action.
- Explain AI assistance and apply the same principles to a different task.
- Deliver a bounded AI-assisted workflow with evidence of independent judgment.

## Delivery and time
Estimated 26.5 hours of learning and practice, excluding reviewer waiting and additional revisions. Suggested pace: 4–6 weeks at approximately 4.5–7 hours per week, adjustable to learner availability. These are planning estimates, not validated completion times. Sixteen short lessons, sixteen formative checks, seven portfolio outputs, and one capstone.

Text-first, phone-compatible exercises. A notes editor and browser are sufficient for reading, drafting, and arithmetic checks. An approved AI tool is needed to demonstrate actual tool use; no paid subscription is prescribed. Offline learners can critique supplied drafts and prepare evidence, but must later demonstrate any tool-use competency being claimed. Camera optional; accept English, Filipino, or Taglish with equivalent clarity.

## Learning process
Read → explain in your own words → try a synthetic case → check reasoning → produce evidence → receive criterion-specific feedback → revise → demonstrate transfer → authorized mastery decision.

Both unit checks must pass before work can enter the local review queue. The whole handbook remains readable for accessibility and preparation. Submission and practice completion are not mastery. Formal prerequisite waivers require an authorized review of CEE evidence, not self-selection or a browser flag.

## Assessment
Every criterion must score at least 2. No averaging away a safety or verification failure. Practice checks do not award mastery. Final decisions require an authorized reviewer, versioned evidence, and a recorded rationale.

- 0: Missing or unsafe: no usable evidence, or a material uncontrolled risk.
- 1: Developing: partly demonstrated but important corrections needed.
- 2: Competent: accurate, usable evidence with a clear explanation and appropriate controls.
- 3: Transfer-ready: meets level 2 and adapts the method to a changed case with justified decisions.

Each unit has four observable criteria in its worksheet. Seven unit outputs must pass before final capstone submission. The capstone includes three tests (normal, missing-data, unsafe/conflicting input), a changed-condition response, and a written or approved audio defense.

Feedback identifies the criterion, evidence, gap, correction, and retry task. Preserve attempts; re-review the revised evidence. Escalate repeated difficulty to support without diagnosing the learner.

## Scope and limits
This pathway develops AI literacy and entry-level applied AI workflow judgment. It does not establish AI engineering, programming, autonomous deployment, legal/medical expertise, employment eligibility, or guaranteed income. Credentials remain private Cognita training records, subject to production verification and institutional approval.
